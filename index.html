About

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at Nagoya City University. My research focuses on international trade policy, structural gravity, and applied general equilibrium analysis. I study how tariffs, regional trade agreements, and global value chain linkages affect trade and welfare, using both econometric and simulation-based approaches.
